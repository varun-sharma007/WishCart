03 / Plan

Lean to launch. Built to grow.

PHASE 01

Foundation

Next.js storefront on Vercel, Spring Boot API in Docker, and Supabase for database, authentication, and image storage.

PHASE 02

Admin control

Secure multi-admin access; complete product, deal, store, category, price-range, ranking, logo, and landing-content management.

PHASE 03

Customer experience

Responsive home page, the three shopping areas, product details, filters, search, redirects, and visitor product suggestions.

PHASE 04

Launch ready

CSV product import/export, SEO, expired-deal handling, analytics, policies, and effortless switching to affiliate links later.

Why it works

Curated, not crowded.

  • Useful recommendations instead of overwhelming marketplaces
  • Time-sensitive deal discovery creates reason to return
  • Trusted retailers handle checkout, payment, and delivery
How it stays manageable

Everything is admin-managed.

  • No developer needed to add or update products
  • Products can belong to several sections and price ranges
  • Affiliate links can replace standard links at any time
